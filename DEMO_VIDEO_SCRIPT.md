## 🎯 Simple 2-Minute Script

### **SEGMENT 1: Introduction (0:00 - 0:20) - 20 seconds**

**Script:**
> "Hi! This is HealthyChef's invoice system. We make healthy eating easy since 2019. This app manages our menu, creates invoices, and generates PDFs. Let me show you."

**Actions:**
- Show homepage
- Point to 3 tabs: Items, Create Invoice, Dashboard

---

### **SEGMENT 2: Add Menu Item (0:20 - 0:40) - 20 seconds**

**Script:**
> "First, I'll add a menu item - our Quinoa Power Bowl."

**Actions:**
1. Fill form quickly:
   - Name: "Quinoa Power Bowl"
   - Description: "Healthy quinoa with chicken and veggies"
   - Price: "299"
2. Click "Add Item"
3. Show it in the list

---

### **SEGMENT 3: Create Invoice (0:40 - 1:20) - 40 seconds**

**Script:**
> "Now let's create an invoice. I'll add customer details and select items. Watch the totals calculate automatically."

**Actions:**
1. Click "Create Invoice" tab
2. Fill customer info fast:
   - Name: "Sarah Johnson"
   - Phone: "+91 98765 43210"
   - Email: "sarah@email.com"
   - Address: "123 MG Road, Mumbai"
3. Click "Add Item"
4. Select "Quinoa Power Bowl"
5. Quantity: "2"
6. Point to live calculations on right

---

### **SEGMENT 4: Save & PDF (1:20 - 1:45) - 25 seconds**

**Script:**
> "I'll save this invoice and download the PDF."

**Actions:**
1. Click "Save Invoice"
2. Show success message
3. Click "Download PDF"
4. Open PDF quickly
5. Scroll through it

---

### **SEGMENT 5: Dashboard (1:45 - 2:00) - 15 seconds**

**Script:**
> "The dashboard shows all invoices and revenue. Built with MongoDB, Express, React, and Node.js. That's HealthyChef's invoice system!"

**Actions:**
1. Click "Invoice Dashboard"
2. Show statistics
3. Show invoice table
4. Done!

## 🎓 Pro Tips

1. **Practice First**: Do 2-3 dry runs before recording
2. **Script Nearby**: Have this script visible while recording
3. **Smooth Movements**: Move cursor slowly and deliberately
4. **Pause Between Sections**: Makes editing easier
5. **Backup Recording**: Record twice in case of issues
6. **Check Audio**: Test audio before full recording
7. **Natural Pace**: Don't rush, speak naturally
8. **Show, Don't Tell**: Let the UI speak for itself
9. **Brand Focus**: Emphasize HealthyChef's mission of healthy eating
10. **Food Appeal**: Make the menu items sound delicious!
