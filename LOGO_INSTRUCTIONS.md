# Logo Setup Instructions

## 📁 Save Your Logo

Please save your company logo to the following location:

**Path:** `client/public/assets/logo.png`

### Steps:
1. Save the logo image you shared as `logo.png`
2. Place it in: `client/public/assets/`
3. The application will automatically use it in PDFs

---

## 🖼️ Supported Formats:
- PNG (recommended - transparent background)
- JPG/JPEG
- SVG (if needed)

## 📐 Recommended Size:
- Width: 200-300px
- Height: Auto (maintains aspect ratio)
- Resolution: 300 DPI for best PDF quality

---

## ✅ After Saving:
The logo will automatically appear in:
1. PDF invoices (top left corner)
2. Next to company name in header
3. All generated documents
