### Invoice Structure

The invoice template includes the following sections:

#### 1. Header
- Company logo and name
- Business tagline
- GSTIN number

#### 2. Invoice Details (Top Right)
- Tax Invoice Number format: `-XXX/DD-MM-YYYY/YYNK`
- Date format: DD-MM-YYYY

#### 3. Customer Details (TO:- Section)
- Customer name/organization
- Address line 1
- Address line 2 (City/Location)

#### 4. Items Table
Columns:
- **SR. NO.** - Serial number
- **DESCRIPTION** - Item/service description
- **QTY** - Quantity
- **DENO** - Denomination (e.g., pieces, units, sets)
- **RATE** - Price per unit
- **AMOUNT** - Total amount (QTY × RATE)

#### 5. Calculations
- **SUB TOTAL** - Sum of all item amounts
- **GST 18%** - 18% Goods and Services Tax
- **TOTAL** - Subtotal + GST
- **Round off** - Adjustment to nearest rupee

#### 6. Amount in Words
- "IN WORDS:- Rupees [amount in words] Only."
